Privacy Policy

Effective 24 October 2026

This policy explains what the Little Life Village app ("the app") stores, how the optional Google Calendar connection works, and how you can disconnect or delete your data.

1. Information the app handles

  • Account: your email address and password. Passwords are stored only as a one-way (bcrypt) hash. If you sign in with Google, your Google account's name and profile picture are also saved to your account.
  • Content you create: routines, activities, subtasks, schedules, study templates, events and deadlines, meal plans, recipes and recipe photos, grocery lists, routine history, village progress (coins, experience, unlocked items) and settings such as saved music links.
  • Device time zone, so reminders and "today" follow your local day.

2. Google Calendar (optional)

Nothing is read from Google Calendar unless you tap Connect Google Calendar and approve access on Google's own sign-in page. The app requests these permissions:

PermissionWhy the app needs it
https://www.googleapis.com/auth/calendar.readonly
See and download any calendar you can access		To read events from the one calendar you choose to import from (your main calendar by default).
https://www.googleapis.com/auth/calendar.app.created
Make secondary calendars, and see, create, change and delete events on them		Only used if you ask the app to create a separate "Little Life Village Inbox" calendar to import from. The app does not create, change or delete events in any calendar.

How calendar data is used. The app's server reads events from your chosen calendar when you connect, when you open the app or tap Sync now, and automatically about every 10 minutes while the connection and automatic import are on. From each event it reads the title, description, start and end time, location, repeat rule, status and event type. It does not read guest lists or attachments. Events written in the app's import format are turned into routines, activities, events, recipes or grocery items in your account; other upcoming one-off events (such as appointments or deadlines) are imported as events. Past events, repeating events without the import format, and Google's automatic focus-time, out-of-office and working-location blocks are skipped. If an imported event is deleted in Google Calendar, the matching item is archived or set to inactive in the app. Calendar data is used only to provide this import inside the app. It is not sold and not used for advertising.

What is stored. Google's long-lived access token (refresh token) is stored on the app's server, encrypted, and is never sent to your phone. Items imported from your calendar become part of your app data (see section 3). For each imported item the server also keeps the Google calendar ID and event ID, so later changes can be updated in place, and an import log entry (event title, result and a short note), which is deleted automatically after 60 days.

Little Life Village's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

3. Where data is stored

  • On your device: your app data is saved in the app's own storage so it works offline. Your sign-in tokens are kept in the device's secure storage (Android Keystore).
  • On the app's server: your account and a copy of your app data are stored in the app's database so your devices stay in sync. The server is currently hosted by Emergent (cozy-routine-village.emergent.host).

4. How data is protected

  • All connections between the app and its server use HTTPS.
  • Your data on the server is only returned to requests signed in to your account.
  • Passwords are stored as bcrypt hashes; the Google refresh token is encrypted before it is stored.
  • Sign-in sessions use short-lived access tokens (60 minutes) and rotating refresh tokens that are stored only as hashes on the server.

5. Sharing with other services

Your data is not sold. It reaches these services only as needed to run the features you use:

  • Google: the Calendar API (if you connect it) and Google sign-in (if you use it).
  • Emergent: hosts the app's server and database, provides the Google sign-in service the app uses, and stores recipe photos you upload.
  • OpenAI (through Emergent's integration service): turns the companion's spoken lines into audio. Spoken lines can include activity names, reminders and deadline titles, so they can include titles of events imported from your calendar. The generated audio is cached on the app's server.
  • Anthropic (Claude), only if you connect it: the optional Claude connector lets Claude read and change your Little Life Village data, including items imported from your calendar, when you ask it to. It does not give Claude access to your Google account.
  • Spotify and YouTube, only for music you add: music links play in their official embedded players, which load from Spotify or YouTube.

6. Disconnecting Google Calendar and deleting your data

  • Disconnect Google Calendar: in the app, open Settings → Google Calendar → Disconnect Google Calendar. The server deletes its stored token and asks Google to revoke it. Items already imported stay in your app until you delete them. You can also remove access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Delete your account: in the app, open Settings → Account and choose to delete your account. This deletes from the server your account, your synced app data, your running timer, your Google Calendar connection, import links and logs, sign-in sessions and any Claude connector access.
  • Data stored on your phone stays there until you uninstall the app or clear its storage.
  • To ask for deletion of anything not covered above (for example uploaded recipe photos), contact the developer below.
