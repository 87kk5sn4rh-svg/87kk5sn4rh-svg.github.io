A cozy routine timer and planner, with a little village that grows with you

Little Life Village is a mobile app (Android) that guides you through your day: it times your routines, speaks friendly cues, and rewards finished routines with progress in your own village.

Routines and productivity

  • Daily routines made of timed activities, with a 3-2-1 countdown, automatic moves to the next activity, pause, skip and extra time.
  • Subtasks and checklists for each activity; ticking the last subtask finishes the activity early.
  • Schedules: day and week timetables, repeat rules (daily, chosen weekdays, every few days) and one-off changes for single dates.
  • Study templates with focus and break cycles, spoken announcements and optional study music from Spotify or YouTube links you add.
  • Deadlines, events and quick emergency tasks on the Today dashboard, plus wake-up and bedtime timers.
  • Kitchen: meal plans, saved recipes and grocery lists.
  • Your account keeps routines and progress in sync across your devices.

Your village and companion

Finishing routines earns coins and experience. You use them to grow a personal village: unlock buildings, decorations, villagers, pets and collectible characters. A companion character cheers you on and speaks your routine's instructions aloud.

Your village, routines and history are private to your account; they are only visible after you sign in to the app.

Optional Google Calendar integration

If you choose to connect Google Calendar, Little Life Village reads the calendar you pick and imports plans written for the app (for example routines, study sessions, events, meals or grocery lists you add yourself or with an AI assistant) into your account. It does not change or delete events in your existing calendars. You can disconnect at any time.

Details about what is read, stored and shared are in the Privacy Policy.
